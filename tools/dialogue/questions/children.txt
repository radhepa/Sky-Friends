# Questions Cirri children ask a walker. Each is asked once ever; flagged answers are remembered, and their
# callbacks (remember.<flag>) are in lines/remember-children.txt.

? kid_ground_taste | when: child
Q: Have you ever licked the ground? What does it taste of? I need to know. It's important.
Q: Does the ground taste of anything? {friend} says it tastes of boots.
- Rain and dust :: Rain and dust! So the ground is where rain goes to get dusty. I knew it! @ground_taste:rain {points:1} [IDEA]
- Never licked it :: Never? You've stood on it your whole life and never once had a lick? Walkers are very strange. {points:1} [QUESTION]
- Like honey, secretly :: Honey! I knew walkers were keeping the best bit to themselves! @ground_taste:honey {points:1} [EXCLAIM]
