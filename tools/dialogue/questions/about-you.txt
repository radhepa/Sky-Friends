# Questions the Cirri ask about you and the ground below. Each is asked once, ever; flagged answers are
# remembered and called back in lines/remember.txt. No "when:" means anyone may ask, children too, so those
# questions and replies are written so a child could say them (no "walker": children say "groundling").

? where_live | when: adult
Q: Where do you live, walker? Somewhere down under all that cloud?
Q: Do you have a home below? I've always wondered what a walker's nest looks like.
- A house I built myself :: You built it? Stone on stone, with your own hands? No wonder you're so sturdy. @home:built {points:1} [EXCLAIM]
- Wherever night finds me :: A wanderer! You're more like the winds than you know. They never sleep in the same place twice either. @home:wanderer {points:1} [NOTE]
- In a hole in a hill :: Inside the hill? Under the dirt, with the roots and the dark? Oh, you brave, strange thing. @home:burrow {points:1} [SWEAT]
- Up here, if you'll have me :: Here? Oh, walker. Then {village} has one more nest to keep an eye on. @home:sky {points:2} [HEART]

? ever_fallen
Q: Have you ever fallen? From somewhere really, really high?
Q: Tell me true. Have you ever fallen a long, long way?
- More times than I can count :: More than you can count! And still you come up here. That's either very brave or very forgetful. @fell:often {points:1} [SWEAT]
- Once, and never again :: Once is plenty for anyone. You landed in one piece, didn't you? You must have. Here you are. @fell:once {points:1} [DOTS]
- Never. I'm careful :: Careful! Good. The careful ones get to come back and visit. @trait:careful {points:1} [NOTE]
- Only in dreams :: In dreams! The kind where your stomach drops and you wake up kicking? We have those too. @dreams:falling {points:1} [SLEEP]

- Sefa, warm and lazy :: Sefa! Smells of peaches and never in a hurry. You'd doze in the air all afternoon. @likes:sefa {points:1} [NOTE]
- Vell, who carries songs :: Vell, the evening wind. Then you'd always arrive just in time for the Evensong. @likes:vell {points:1} [HEART]
- Borre, cold and wild :: Borre? The storm-bringer? You're a strange one, walker. A strange, brave one. @likes:borre {points:1} [EXCLAIM]
