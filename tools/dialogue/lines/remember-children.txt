## remember.ground_taste_rain
Rain and dust, you said the ground tastes of. Now when it rains, I think of the ground having a drink.
Since you said the ground tastes of rain, half the village has been catching raindrops on their tongues to compare.
So the ground is where the rain goes to get dusty. Everyone's heard. It's a very good fact.

## remember.ground_game_hide_seek

## remember.ground_game_tag

Tag on the ground and tag in the sky. The same game everywhere. That makes the world feel small and friendly.
Tag! You're it! That's because you said walkers play it too.

## remember.ground_game_sandcastles

The weavers want to know how sand stays in a castle shape. So does everyone. You started this.

## remember.ground_game_skipping

Bounce, bounce, plop. That's how stones skip, you said. It's fun to say.
